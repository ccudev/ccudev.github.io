Shorts Publisher — Architecture & User Flow

Private, single-user tool. Operator: owner of the YouTube channel "기기묘묘 이야기". Google Cloud project: shorts-auto.

1. Production (no YouTube API)Scripts are written from verified historical sources; narration and illustrations are generated; video is rendered with ffmpeg.
→
2. Human reviewThe channel owner watches every video and approves or requests changes. Nothing is posted without explicit approval.
→
3. YouTube Data APIvideos.insert (resumable upload, status.privacyStatus=private + publishAt, containsSyntheticMedia=true) → scheduled publish at 21:00 KST.
AuthorizationOAuth 2.0 installed-app (loopback) flow, run once by the owner on their own computer. Scopes: youtube.upload, youtube.readonly. Refresh token stored only in the owner's private environment variables.
Read-only callschannels.list (mine=true) to confirm the connected channel; videos.list (statistics) once per video, 7 days after publishing, to record view counts in the owner's private sheet.

User flow (daily)

  1. The tool renders one Short (~60 s) from the owner's topic list.
  2. The owner receives the video for review and replies "OK" or with edits.
  3. On approval, the tool calls videos.insert once for that video with a scheduled publish time (Mon–Sat 21:00 KST).
  4. 7 days later, the tool calls videos.list to read the view count.
Expected usage: about 1 upload per day (max 6 per week) + a few read calls; well within the default 10,000 units/day. Only the owner's own channel is ever accessed; there are no other users.
